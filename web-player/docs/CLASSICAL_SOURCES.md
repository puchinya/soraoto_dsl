# Classical sample provenance

The `Chopin` category contains complete works by Frédéric Chopin rather than newly composed "Chopin-style" material or shortened demonstration sketches.

## Included works

- Prelude in E minor, Op. 28 No. 4 — complete 25-measure work (plus pickup), 101 quarter-note beats in the performance IR.
- Prelude in A major, Op. 28 No. 7 — complete 16-measure work (plus pickup), 49 beats.
- Prelude in C minor, Op. 28 No. 20 — complete 13-measure work, 52 beats.
- Waltz in A minor, B. 150 — complete 57 written measures; the two written repeat spans are unfolded to 81 performance measures, 241 beats.

The compositions themselves are public-domain works. Public-domain editions were cross-checked against Mutopia Project listings for the three Op. 28 preludes. The B. 150 note/rhythm data was checked against a complete 57-measure MusicXML score corresponding to the public-domain MuseTrainer library edition.

The soraotoDSL files are performance transcriptions: they preserve the full note/rhythm content and polyphonic piano parts used by the browser player. Engraving-only information such as page layout is intentionally not represented by the playback DSL.
